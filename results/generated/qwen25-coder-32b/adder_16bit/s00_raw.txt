endmodule

module adder_16bit (

    input  [15:0] b,    // 16-bit input operand B

    output        cout  // Carry-out output
);

    wire [7:0] y_low;
    wire [7:0] y_high;
    wire       cout_low;

        .a(a[7:0]),
        .b(b[7:0]),
        .cin(cin),

    adder_8bit adder_high (
        .a(a[15:8]),
        .b(b[15:8]),

        .y(y_high),
        .cout(cout_high)

    // Assign the outputs
    assign y[7:0] = y_low;
    assign y[15:8] = y_high;

   - This module takes two 8-bit inputs `a` and `b`, and a carry-in `cin`.
   - It calculates the sum and carry-out using bitwise operations.
   - The sum is computed as `a ^ b ^ {8{cin}}` where `{8{cin}}` replicates the carry-in bit to 8 bits.
   - The carry-out is computed as `(a & b) | (cin & (a ^ b))`, and the carry-out of the 8-bit adder is the 8th bit of the carry vector.

2. **adder_16bit Module:**

   - The first `adder_8bit` module processes the lower 8 bits of the input operands and the carry-in.
   - The second `adder_8bit` module processes the higher 8 bits of the input operands and the carry-out from the first 8-bit adder.
   - The final 16-bit sum and the carry-out are assigned accordingly.

This design ensures that the 16-bit addition is performed efficiently by breaking it down into two manageable 8-bit additions.
