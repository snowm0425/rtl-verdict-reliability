### 4-bit CLA Block

```verilog
module cla_4bit (
    input [3:0] A,
    input [3:0] B,
    input C_in,
    output [3:0] S,
    output C_out,
    output G,
    output P
);

    wire G1, G2, G3;
    wire P1, P2, P3;

    // Generate and Propagate signals for each bit
    assign G1 = A[0] & B[0];
    assign P1 = A[0] | B[0];
    assign G2 = A[1] & B[1];
    assign P2 = A[1] | B[1];
    assign G3 = A[2] & B[2];
    assign P3 = A[2] | B[2];

    // Sum and Carry signals
    assign S[0] = A[0] ^ B[0] ^ C_in;
    assign S[1] = A[1] ^ B[1] ^ (P1 & C_in);
    assign S[2] = A[2] ^ B[2] ^ (P2 & P1 & C_in);
    assign S[3] = A[3] ^ B[3] ^ (P3 & P2 & P1 & C_in);

    // Generate and Propagate for the 4-bit block
    assign G = G3 | (P3 & G2) | (P3 & P2 & G1);
    assign P = P3 & P2 & P1;

    // Carry out for the 4-bit block
    assign C_out = G | (P & C_in);

endmodule
```

### Explanation

1. **4-bit CLA Block (`cla_4bit`)**: This module calculates the sum and carry for 4 bits, along with the generate (`G`) and propagate (`P`) signals used for lookahead carry generation.
2. **16-bit CLA Block (`cla_16bit`)**: This module is composed of four 4-bit CLA blocks. It calculates the sum and carry for 16 bits.
3. **32-bit CLA Adder (`adder_32bit`)**: This module is composed of two 16-bit CLA blocks. It calculates the sum and carry for 32 bits.

This design uses the CLA architecture to significantly reduce the carry propagation delay compared to a ripple-carry adder.
