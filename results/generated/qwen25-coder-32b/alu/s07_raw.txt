    parameter SUB = 6'b100010;

                carry = res[32];
                overflow = (a[31] == b[31]) && (res[31] != a[31]);

                overflow = 1'b0;

### Explanation:

- **res[32:0]**: An intermediate 33-bit register is used to capture the carry out for addition and subtraction.
- **always @(*)**: A combinational block to perform the operations based on `aluc`.
- **Operations**: Each operation is handled in the `case` statement. For signed operations (ADD, SUB), carry and overflow flags are calculated.
- **Flags**:

  - `negative` is set if the MSB of the result is 1.
  - `flag` is set to 1 for SLT and SLTU, and high-impedance for other operations.
