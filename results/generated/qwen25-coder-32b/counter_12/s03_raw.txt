```verilog
    end

endmodule
```

### Explanation:
- **Reset Signal (`rst_n`)**: The counter is reset to `4'b0000` whenever `rst_n` is low (active reset).
- **Clock Signal (`clk`)**: The counter logic is triggered on the rising edge of the clock signal.
- **Valid Count Signal (`valid_count`)**: 
  - When `valid_count` is high (1), the counter increments on each clock cycle.
  - If the counter reaches the maximum value (`4'd11`), it wraps around and resets to `4'b0000`.
  - If `valid_count` is low (0), the counter remains unchanged.
- **Output (`out`)**: A 4-bit register that holds the current count value.
