                        next_state <= STATE_4;

2. **Counter**: The counter keeps track of the number of clock cycles within each state.
3. **Intermediate Clocks**: `clk_div_int_4` and `clk_div_int_3` are generated based on the counter reaching the end of their respective periods.
4. **Phase Shifting**: The intermediate clocks are phase-shifted by one clock cycle using a simple flip-flop (`clk_div_phase_shifted_4` and `clk_div_phase_shifted_3`).
5. **Final OR**: The final fractional clock (`clk_div`) is generated by logically OR-ing the two phase-shifted intermediate clocks.
