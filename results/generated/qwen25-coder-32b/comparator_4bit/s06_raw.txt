    A_less = borrow;
end

- **Borrow Signal:** The `borrow` signal indicates whether a borrow occurs at each bit position during the subtraction. If a borrow occurs at the most significant bit (MSB), it means `A` is less than `B`.
- **Comparison Logic:**
