```verilog
module LFSR (
    input clk,          // Clock signal
    input rst,          // Active high reset signal

        out <= 4'b0000;
    end else begin
        // Calculate the feedback bit as the inverted XOR of out[3] and out[2]
        reg feedback;
        feedback = ~(out[3] ^ out[2]);
        
        // Shift the register left and insert the feedback bit at the LSB
        out <= {out[2:0], feedback};
    end
end

endmodule
```

2. **Sensitivity List**: The `always` block is sensitive to both the rising edge of the clock (`posedge clk`) and the rising edge of the reset (`posedge rst`).
3. **Reset Condition**: When `rst` is high, the register `out` is initialized to `4'b0000`.
4. **Feedback Calculation**: The feedback bit is calculated as the inverted XOR of the most significant bit (`out[3]`) and the second most significant bit (`out[2]`).
5. **Shifting Operation**: On the rising edge of the clock and when `rst` is low, the bits in the register are shifted left, and the feedback bit is inserted at the least significant bit (LSB).

This design ensures that the LFSR generates a pseudo-random sequence based on the specified feedback mechanism.
